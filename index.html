Who We Are

Average Build.
Real Passion.

Motion Workz started with one goal — get to the GT-R. But the real story is everything in between. Based in Georgia, we document everyday JDM and import builds from people who actually drive their cars.

No trailer queens. No perfect builds. Just real enthusiasts putting in the work and enjoying the ride.

The merch keeps the content going. The content tells the stories that don't get told — the average build that took two years, the car that barely made it to the meet, the drive that reminded you why you got into this in the first place.

“That's Motion Workz.”

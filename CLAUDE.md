# Motion Workz — Shopify Theme

## Brand Overview
Motion Workz is a JDM and import car lifestyle brand based in Georgia.
Solo founder. Sells clothing (tees, hoodies) to fund car content creation.
Tagline: "The Journey Is the Build."
Hero car: 1999 Nissan Skyline GT-T (Right Hand Drive). Dream car: 2001 Nissan GT-R.

## Brand Colors
- Primary accent: #CC1111 (red)
- Background: #0A0A0A (black)
- Text / Chrome: #E8E8E8 (silver-white)
- Secondary text: #888888 (gray)

## Typography
- Headlines: Barlow Condensed, 800 weight, uppercase
- Body: Barlow, 400–500 weight

## Target Customer
JDM and import car enthusiasts of all ages.
Real builders, not show-car collectors.
Southeast US + national JDM community.
Motto: Driven Not Trailered.
